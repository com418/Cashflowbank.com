About Cashflowbank

Money management should feel simple.

Cashflowbank is being designed around an easy-to-understand digital banking experience. The website combines a modern landing page with a signed-in dashboard and Firebase-powered account access.

  • ✓Clear dashboard sections for overview, activity, cards, savings, and settings.
  • ✓Account creation date read from Firebase Authentication metadata.
  • ✓Responsive emerald-green and lime-green design.
  • ✓Demo transactions are labeled as examples, not real bank records.
